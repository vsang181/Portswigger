# Brute-forcing passwords

Passwords can be brute-forced using automated tools and wordlists, with the difficulty depending directly on the password's complexity and randomness (**entropy**).

## Common Password Policy Defenses

To make passwords harder to crack through brute-force attacks alone, many websites enforce strict password policies requiring:

- **Minimum length**: A required minimum number of characters (for example, 8 to 12+ characters).

- **Character variety**: A mix of both uppercase and lowercase letters, often alongside numbers.

- **Special characters**: At least one symbol or non-alphanumeric character (such as `!`, `@`, `#`, or `$`).
